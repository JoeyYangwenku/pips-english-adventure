## What makes Pip different?

| Feature / 特色 | Value / 学习价值 |
|---|---|
| **Story with purpose** 故事有目标 | Help Pip restore the Crystal Bridge through six short English activities; the story motivates, but learning remains central. |
| **Progressive practice** 循序渐进 | Listen and identify → follow instructions → two-item listening → CVC word → simple sentence → reading comprehension. |
| **Live learning evidence** 动态学习记录 | Lesson Map and Learning Evidence react to choices, wrong attempts, retries, first-try correctness and completion. |
| **Parent-friendly insight** 中文家长专区 | Displays what was practiced and where review may help, without equating stars with mastery. |
| **Safe prototype design** 适合家庭试用 | No account required; no speech recording or open-ended AI chatbot. |

## Unit 1 — The Lost Crystal

Six guided learning activities support early English listening, vocabulary, letter awareness, sentence formation and reading comprehension. The crystal bridge and six stars represent **task progress**, not validated long-term proficiency.

**Current limitations:** The voice is browser text-to-speech and varies by device; phonics is introductory rather than a complete phonics curriculum; no robust speech scoring, longitudinal evaluation or independently demonstrated learning gains. Character art and concept imagery are prototype assets and not finalized commercial production assets.

## Product principles

- **Learning first:** meaningful English tasks, not excessive game mechanics.
- **English for children, Chinese for parents.**
- **Feedback rooted in evidence:** record attempts without overstating mastery.
- **Age-appropriate interaction:** short activities, optional hints, no punitive rewards.
- **Preserve the Crystal Bridge story** across versions.
